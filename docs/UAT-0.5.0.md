# UAT — app 0.5.0 (look & feel)

Install ASTRA-v0.5.0.apk over 0.4.0 (your data stays). Try on a phone and, if you have one, a tablet or foldable. Tick each line or note what you saw.

## Everywhere
- [ ] The background sky moves gently (stars twinkle, occasional glimmer or shooting star). Nothing flickers or feels busy.
- [ ] With Settings → Accessibility → Remove animations on, the sky and card shimmer stop.
- [ ] Light theme (parchment) and dark theme both read clearly.
- [ ] Rotate the phone: nothing is cut off. On a tablet, a side rail replaces the bottom bar.

## Today
- [ ] Today's tarot card and today's guidance card both show; tap to turn each over; they stay the same all day.
- [ ] "Ask about this card" opens Ask with the card in the question.

## Tarot
- [ ] Classic tarot cards are the familiar 1909 pictures, easy to recognise (e.g. The Fool, Death, Three of Swords).
- [ ] Draw: the deck shuffles, then fans out. Pick 3 cards by tapping; picked cards glow. "Choose for me" also works.
- [ ] Turn cards over one by one and with "Turn all over"; meanings appear.
- [ ] Tarot reading and Cross-Reading work as in 0.4.0; save and rate.
- [ ] Yes / No, Guidance, Vibe and Nakshatra decks each draw a card and turn it over.

## Tracker
- [ ] The sky wheel shows the planets; your Moon sign is highlighted and named in the centre.
- [ ] Timeline and Chart tabs work; on a tablet the chart is a sensible size with Lagna visible below it.

## Me
- [ ] Credits mention the 1909 deck and the Cinzel font.
