# UAT checklist — ASTRA 0.4.0 (M4)

Install the APK from the latest release over 0.3.0 (your details stay).

1. **Tabs.** The bar reads Today · Tracker · Tarot · Ask · Me. Your chart is now Tracker → Chart. Is it easy to find?
2. **Tarot.** Type a real question, choose Three cards, draw. Turn the cards over one by one. Do the cards look right on your phone (names readable, art crisp, reversed cards upside down)? Read the meanings.
3. **Ask ASTRA to read these cards.** Does it speak about the cards only, not your planets?
4. **Cross-Reading.** Are all five parts there and is "Where they differ" honest? Rate it, save it, find it under Saved → Cross-Readings with the cards shown.
5. **Today's card.** Turn it over. Close and reopen the app: same card today? Tomorrow: a new one?
6. **Check-in** (quick test): Me → My Story → Check-ins → Every week, then add something on your mind. It will ask in a week; I can also trigger one for you. On Today, answer it and add it as a life event.
7. **Life events.** Add a past event in Me → My Story (e.g. a move in 2019) and a recent one. Tracker → Timeline: the recent one shows as a marker; both are in "Your life events". Tap one: does "what your chart showed then" make sense? Try "Ask about this".
8. **Add to My Story?** Ask about something your story doesn't mention (e.g. a money question). Does the offer appear, and does nothing get added unless you tap Add?
9. **Share my story → PDF.** Send it to yourself. Is it something you'd give an astrologer?
10. **Me → Privacy & data.** Read the notice. Send yourself feedback ("test"). Switch usage stats off and on.
11. **Delete my account** (do this last, or on a second phone): everything should go and the app should start fresh.

Also check the public privacy page: https://astra-ai.app-support-sg.workers.dev/privacy

Send all feedback together; I'll fix everything in one build.
