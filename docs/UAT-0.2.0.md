# UAT checklist — ASTRA 0.2.0 (M2)

Install the APK from the latest GitHub release on your phone. Try these in order:

1. **Onboarding with your own details.** Search your birthplace by the name you'd normally type (e.g. "Bombay", "Bangalore", a small home town). Is it found? Does the time zone shown look right?
2. **Chart against a chart you trust.** Compare Lagna, Moon sign, birth nakshatra and pada, and each planet's sign with a kundli you already have (Jagannatha Hora or a family astrologer's). Note any difference.
3. **Dasha dates.** Do your current mahadasha and antardasha, and their dates, match what you've been told before?
4. **Graha Tracker → Now.** Read "Where you are". Is it clear what the tone means and why? Tap Saturn and Jupiter.
5. **Timeline.** Scroll sideways, tap a few bands. Is it understandable without help?
6. **Today.** Check tithi and Rahu Kaal against a panchang you use (Drik Panchang, a calendar).
7. **Edit details** in Profile and change the birth time to "I don't know". The chart should switch to a Moon chart and stop showing a Lagna.
8. **Light mode** (Profile → Appearance). Anything hard to read?

Send all feedback together; I'll fix everything in one build.
