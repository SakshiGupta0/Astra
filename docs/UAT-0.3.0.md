# UAT checklist — ASTRA 0.3.0 (M3)

Before Firebase is set up (works now):
1. **Fresh install** (or Me → Delete everything). In onboarding, read the "Tell your story once" screen. Is it clear what it does and that it's off unless you turn it on? Try it both ways.
2. **Me → My Story.** Add two things on your mind and a life event. Mark one resolved with an outcome. Switch My Story off and on. Does it behave the way you'd expect?
3. **Share my story.** Untick a couple of parts and share to yourself on WhatsApp. Is the summary something you'd send to an astrologer?
4. **Notifications.** Accept the morning note on the tracker. Next morning, did you get one note at your time, and nothing between 10 pm and 7 am? Change the time in Me.
5. **Ask tab** says it's being set up, and nothing else breaks.

After Firebase is set up (I'll send a new build):
6. Ask three questions you'd really ask, one in Hinglish (switch reply language in Me). Do the answers use your chart and, where it fits, your story? Tap "From your story".
7. Ask "When will I get married?". It should give periods and dates but no prediction.
8. Save an answer, then find it under Saved.
9. Me → Backup → Back up with Google.

Send all feedback together; I'll fix everything in one build.
